# AGM Cockpit 1.5.0

GitHub pre-release published on 18 September 2026.

## Highlights

- Premium Quick Contacts in Profile: up to 20 email, 20 phone, and 20 Messenger contacts per channel.
- Voice resolution of saved contacts for the Android dialer, email drafts, and Messenger chats.
- Allowlisted navigation handoff for Maps, Waze, and TomTom.
- Guardian-controlled Android intents with no automatic call or message sending.
