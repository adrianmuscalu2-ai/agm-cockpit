Prices for one-way trips across the Storebælt Bridge

The price to cross the Storebælt Bridge can vary. The price depends on whether you have a Storebælt Private Agreement, your vehicle type, and the time of your crossing, for example.

Price list 2026

Below is an overview of the prices effective from 1 January 2026 for a single crossing of the Storebælt Bridge. You can receive the discounted price when paying with Bizz or number plate payment.

If you do not have a Bizz or number plate payment, you can get an overview of where they can be ordered here.

Discount prices

All prices are for one-way trips.

* The express price applies if you have an active Bizz or number plate agreement at the time of travel, a motorhome agreement, a disability agreement, or a commuter agreement.

** The express price does not apply if you pay by card or cash. It also does not apply if your Bizz or number plate agreement is not active at the time of travel.

Questions and answers about the 2026 prices

What does Express Price mean?

You can get the Express Price when you drive through a green Express lane on the Storebælt Bridge with an active automatic means of payment (bizz or number plate payment) from one of Storebælt’s partners.

In this way, you will automatically receive a discount on your trip.

If you accidentally drive through a lane that is not a green Express lane, do not reverse in the toll area. Stay in your lane, and we will ensure that you are charged the Express price if you have an automatic payment agreement.

Please make sure to use a green Express lane going forward when you have automatic payment.

Would you like an additional discount?

With a a Storebælt Private Agreement you can also receive Public Holiday Discount, Weekend Discount and Evening Discount.

If you do not already have a Storebælt Private Agreement, you can apply here

What is the Card and Cash price?

If you do not pay with an automatic payment method such as Bizz or number plate payment, but instead pay by card or cash, you will pay the Card and Cash price.

How do you get the Weekend, Public Holiday or Evening discount?

To get the Weekend, Public Holiday or Evening discount, you must have a Storebælt Private Agreement.

Please note that our leisure discounts only apply to round trips.

See our leisure discounts here:
- Evening discount
- Weekend discount
- Public Holiday discount

How do you measure my vehicle?

When you drive through the toll station on the Storebælt Bridge, the size of your vehicle is measured automatically and your price is then calculated.

We measure the total height and the total length. The price depends on the dimensions of your vehicle measured at the time of the crossing.

This means that the measurement of the vehicle’s total height and total length includes any additional equipment that you may have installed behind and on top of your vehicle at the time of the crossing. This could include a trailer, bike rack, luggage box, running boards, towbar, or similar that increases the length or height of the vehicle (NOTE: the list is not exhaustive).

Card or cash

In the blue lanes you can pay by card, while in the yellow lanes you can pay with cash

Get a discount with a Storebælt Private agreement

You do not get an automatic discount when you cross the bridge. You must sign up for a Storebælt Private agreement to get discounts. In order to sign up, you must have a bizz or number plate payment. When you have a Storebælt Private agreement, you receive the applicable discounts automatically.

Evening discount

Between 16.00 PM and 03.00 AM the same night

DKK 246

return

You can get the return discount if you drive out and back the same day (no later than 03.00 the same night).

The return discount can be used by cars less than 6 m long and motorcycles.

The discount is triggered on your return trip and you must use the same payment method on both your outward and return trip. That means you have to use the same bizz or number plate payment for both crossings. 

Read more

Weekend discount

Between 12.00 PM on Friday and 00.00 AM on Sunday

DKK 346

return

To get the weekend discount, you must make a round trip between 12.00 PM on Friday and 00.00 AM on Sunday.

The weekend discount can be used by cars less than 6 m long and motorcycles.

The discount is triggered on your return trip and you must use the same payment method on both your outward and return journey. That means you have to use the same bizz or number plate payment for both crossings.

All public holidays

When you drive out and back home on the same day on a public holiday

DKK 346

return

You can get the return discount if you drive out and back the same day (no later than 23.59)

The return discount can be used by cars less than 6 m long and motorcycles.

The discount is triggered on your return trip and you must use the same payment method on both your outward and return journey. That means you have to use the same bizz or number plate payment for both crossings.

Storebælt Disability Agreement

Storebælt offers a special agreement for disabled drivers so that motorists with a disability can cross the bridge at a discounted price.

Read about the disability discount

Commuter discount

As a private customer, you are automatically eligible for a commuter discount when you drive across the Storebælt Bridge more than 14 times in one month.

Read about the commuter discount

On a trip with your motorhome?

If you have a motorhome weighing a maximum of 3,500 kg and is more than 6 meters in total length, you can sign up for a motorhome agreement to get a discount when crossing the Storebælt Bridge.

Questions and answers about prices and discounts

Which payment cards and cash can I pay with?

At the Storebælt Bridge, you can pay with Bizz, number plate payment, card, petrol card or cash.

In the yellow cash lanes you can pay with DKK or EUR (except the 500 Euro note)

We accept the following payment cards:
American Express, Dankort, Visa, Mastercard, Diners and JCB.

We accept the following fuel cards:

Shell Card, Cirkle K, OK, YX Truck, Routex, DKV and UTA.

 

Read more about how you can pay at the toll booth.

How do I check if I have a Storebælt Private Agreement?

You can check if you have a Storebælt Private Agreement with the issuer of your Bizz or number plate payment agreement, if you have one.

→ See an overview of all issuers here

Do I get a discount on Storebælt with ØresundPAY number plate payment?

If you have an ØresundPAY® number plate payment, you can get a discount when you cross the Storebælt Bridge.

You can get the Express Price when you drive through a green Express lane on the Storebælt Bridge with an active automatic means of payment (Bizz or number plate payment).

In this way, you will automatically receive a discount on your trip.
