Which roads are included?

Find out where you will have to pay toll on this page.

A map of where to pay toll

As of 1 January 2025 trucks of 12 tons and above will pay toll for driving on the Danish state road network and parts of the Danish municipal road network (approx. 10,900 km). This is estimated to cover approx. 75 percent of the total number of driven kilometres by trucks.

On the map, you can zoom in and see the tolled road network:

The map is a digital representation of the segments in the law

Sund & Bælt's digital map shows the current tolled road segments in Denmark. The map is a digital representation of the segments defined in the law (Act No. 1489 of 10 December 2024, on the amendment of, among other things, the law on road toll). Therefore, the segments may differ visually on the map. The legal definition of segments is always the prevailing one.

GNSS equiment must be switched on at all times

On the tolled road network, you must pay for all road segments you drive on. A road segment is a section of a road, for example, between two intersections, or a specific stretch of a longer road.

If you have GNSS equipment in your truck, it must be switched on at all times when driving on the entire Danish road network and not just when driving on tolled roads.

It is the intention of the Danish government that the road toll will apply to the entire Danish public road network (approx. 75,000 km) as of 1 January 2028. 

Get an answer to your question

Do I have to pay toll to drive on the Storebælt Bridge and the Øresund Bridge?

No, you do not have to pay KM toll for driving your truck on the Storebælt Bridge or the Øresund Bridge, but you still have to pay for crossing the bridge as you do today.

Do you drive on the same road segment multiple times?

You will only pay once within 24 hours for the road segment from which you load goods onto your vehicle or on which you live, provided you have not driven on other tolled roads during that same 24-hour period.

An example: you drive into a garage, unload for 10 minutes, and pull out again.

If you return to the garage later in the day, you will pay for the road segment once more.

If you park for 48 hours and the leave, you will pay for the road segment twice as more than 24 hours have passed, since you entered the segment.
