Services for transport levies

On this page, you will find information about the Heavy Vehicle Fee (HVF) and the e-vignette for driving on Swiss motorways.

Via – Pay lump-sum heavy vehicle charge (PSVA) online

The Via Portal can be used to pay the lump-sum heavy vehicle charge (PSVA) for vehicles that are registered outside Switzerland or the Principality of Liechtenstein, regardless of time and place.

More about «Via – Pay lump-sum heavy vehicle charge (PSVA) online»

E-vignette

The electronic toll sticker (e-vignette) can be obtained via www.e-vignette.ch from the «Via Portal» of the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (FOCBS).
