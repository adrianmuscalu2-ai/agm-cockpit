Who must purchase the electronic vignette?

All vehicles with four wheels and a maximum weight of 3.5 tons have an obligation to purchase an electronic vignette.

The obligation does not include towed vehicles and motorcycles, although the list of exempt vehicles will be expanded as of January 1, 2021.
