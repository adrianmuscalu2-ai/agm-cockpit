Vehicles

Passenger cars and minibuses

Diesel-powered passenger cars and minibuses must have a particulate filter or be Euro 5 for legal use in low emission zones.

Rules for passenger cars and minibuses

Vans

Older diesel-powered vans must have a particulate filter or be Euro 5 for legal use in low emission zones. This applies to vans, which are used for both private and commercial purposes.

Rules for vans

Lorries and buses

Older diesel-powered lorries and busses must have particulate filter or be Euro VI for legal use in the low emission zones.

Rules for lorries and buses

The level of the fixed-penalty notice

For lorries and buses the fixed-penalty notice for driving illegally in a low emission zone is DKK 12,500.

For vans, passenger cars and minibuses the fixed-penalty notice is DKK 1,500. 

The Danish Environment Protection Agency is responsible for enforcing the low-emission zones.

Where are the low emission zones?
