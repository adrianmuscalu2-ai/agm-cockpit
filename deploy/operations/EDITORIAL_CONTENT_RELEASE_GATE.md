# Editorial content release gate

No blog, newsroom, press/news feed or journalistic-editorial route may be enabled unless a release record confirms:

- content owner and editorial purpose;
- publication frequency and public-opinion relevance;
- §18(2) MStV applicability review;
- eligible responsible person and Impressum update when applicable;
- Product Owner and legal approval.

Missing evidence means the editorial route remains disabled/noindex.
