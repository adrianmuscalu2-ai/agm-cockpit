# Owner mandate — AGM Library and Car Mover archive consolidation

Document ID: `AGM-CM-MANDATE-2026-08-29`
Version: `1.0`
Date: `2026-08-29`
Status: `CURRENT`
Source: direct Product Owner mandate in the active AGM workspace session
Relationship: authorizes `AGM-LIB-CENTRAL-001`

The Product Owner authorized a distinct AGM Central Librarian and ordered a
non-destructive consolidation of all existing Car Mover architecture,
governance, implementation, API, lifecycle, Job File, OCR, routing, toll,
field, Opportunity Intelligence, Copilot, incident, test, Android, Browser/Web,
report, evidence, protocol and operational-document sources.

The mandate explicitly excludes Production, deployment, runtime logic, schema
changes and any modification of the existing Basic Librarian.

Original sources and historical evidence must remain preserved. Conflicts are
escalated to Owner/Inspector review and never reconciled automatically.
