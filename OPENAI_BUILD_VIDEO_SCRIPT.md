# OpenAI Build Demo Script

Target length: under 3 minutes.

## 1. Opening

Say:

"AGM is an AI-assisted vehicle transport management system. For OpenAI Build, I am presenting AGM Basic, the stable and validated version of the project."

## 2. Show the Home Screen

Show the AGM Basic home screen and briefly mention:

- translation
- OCR
- voice dictation and playback
- Email Assistant
- operational cockpit

Keep this section short. The goal is to establish that the baseline is live, coherent, and ready for practical use.

## 3. Show Core Flow

Demonstrate one fast path through the product:

1. open the translator
2. show a translation result
3. show OCR capture or history
4. show voice input or playback
5. open Email Assistant or the cockpit area

If time allows, point out that the same baseline is intended to remain stable across web and Android packaging.

## 4. Mention Stability

Say:

"The demo is based on the Basic baseline. Premium is being developed separately so the official presentation stays stable and predictable."

## 5. Closing

Say:

"AGM is built to stay practical, traceable, and ready for real operational use. Thank you."

## Visual Notes

- Keep cursor movement slow and deliberate.
- Avoid switching into Premium during the demo.
- Prefer short, clear transitions.
- Keep the screen uncluttered.
- End on the home screen or a clean operational page, not on a settings or admin view.
