# AGM Operational Standard — Maintenance, Quality and Evolution

Status: Official and permanent  
Effective date: 2026-07-16  
Owner: AGM Department — Maintenance, Quality and Evolution

## Mission

Maintain platform stability, preserve accumulated experience, prevent repeated errors,
and ensure that every validated implementation becomes reusable AGM knowledge.

## Official structure

- **Atlas (Codex), Technical Director and Maintenance Coordinator** — architecture,
  priorities, controlled implementation, and final technical approval.
- **Inspector, Quality Control Director** — independent validation, functional audit,
  regression testing, and pre-release verification.
- **Turn Command Center, Operations Director** — monitoring, incident lifecycle,
  intervention coordination, validation tracking, and operational archive.
- **AGM Chronicler, Operational Memory Director** — chronology, decisions, lessons
  learned, solution history, executive reports, and stability statistics.
- **Linguistic Librarian, Reusable Knowledge Director** — message library,
  professional terminology, validated multilingual templates, and knowledge reuse.

## Mandatory lifecycle

1. Record the incident, proposal, or decision with a unique identifier.
2. Atlas analyzes impact, dependencies, and technical priority.
3. Implement the approved change within the authorized scope.
4. Inspector performs independent automated checks and regression audit.
5. A real-world test and human validation are recorded.
6. Turn moves the record to Validated only when all evidence is present.
7. The AGM Chronicler records the decision and lessons learned.
8. Reusable solutions or content are promoted to an AGM standard or library only after
   human approval.

## Separation of duties

- Technical approval does not replace QA or human validation.
- An implementer does not independently validate their own change.
- Turn manages status but cannot declare a remediation valid without evidence.
- The Chronicler cannot rewrite or delete validated history.
- The Linguistic Librarian cannot publish content automatically.
- Reopened incidents preserve every prior event and validation record.

## Official rules

- No resolved error is lost.
- No validated solution is reinvented.
- No important decision remains undocumented.
- A change is not complete until remediation, testing, human validation, and
  documentation are all recorded.
- Every release must be at least as stable and auditable as its predecessor.

This standard must be reviewed whenever department authority, validation gates,
release procedures, or operational-memory storage changes.
