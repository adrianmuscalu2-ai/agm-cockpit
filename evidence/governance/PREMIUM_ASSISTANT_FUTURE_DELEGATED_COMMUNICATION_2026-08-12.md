# Product direction amendment — future delegated communication

Date: 2026-08-12  
Component: `Vorbește cu AGM`

Current stage remains `CONVERSATIONAL / READ-ONLY`.

Future Email and WhatsApp actions must follow:

`USER REQUEST → PREPARE → PREVIEW → HUMAN REVIEW / EDIT → EXPLICIT CONFIRM → SEND → RECEIPT / RESULT`

The assistant never receives autonomous operational authority. Reconnect cannot send an unconfirmed preparation, an ambiguous recipient must be confirmed by the user, and every send must retain channel, recipient, content, confirmation time, result and correlation evidence where supported.

This amendment authorizes product direction only. Gmail, WhatsApp, real sending and Car Mover implementation remain not authorized in the current stage.
